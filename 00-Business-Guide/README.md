# Pharmville Business Guide

Pharmville brings customer ordering, pharmacy review, stock, local fulfillment, delivery and finance into one connected process. This guide explains the intended business in everyday language. The underlying product requirements are inherited drafts; the [decision register](../09-References/Sources-and-Decisions.md) identifies what still needs approval.

Start with the [complete business flow](Pharmville-Complete-Business-Flow.md), then use [the customer experience](Customer-Experience.md) to follow the screens and choices.

Each domain page explains its normal steps, exceptions, responsible team, visible outcome and pending approvals, followed by technical links. These pages cover all 18 existing module groups. Future healthcare, insurance, membership and marketplace tables do not create approved launch journeys; see [scope](../01-Project/Requirements-and-Scope.md#scope-by-phase-and-evidence-level).

Business Flow explains what people do. [HLD](../02-Architecture/System-HLD.md) explains the system boundaries. [LLD](../03-Modules/README.md) explains implementation. The [master feature register](../09-References/Coverage-and-Traceability.md) connects those layers without maintaining another feature inventory.
