Pharmacist Review database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Clinical review decisions and evidence; M/O-only orders do not enter mandatory pharmacist review. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
