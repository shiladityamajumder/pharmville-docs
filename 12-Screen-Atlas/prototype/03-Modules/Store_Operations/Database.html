Store Operations database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Store pick/pack and preparation tasks; Inventory commits stock and Delivery owns custody after handoff. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
