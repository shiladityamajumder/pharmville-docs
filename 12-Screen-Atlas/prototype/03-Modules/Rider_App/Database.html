Rider Operations database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Rider client workflows; no independent direct table write ownership or clinical/finance authority. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.

Proposed owned tables

This client/projection module has no independently owned authoritative tables. It delegates commands to the owners below.
