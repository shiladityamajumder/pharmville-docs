This register lists every recorded foreign-key constraint owned by child tables in the finance schema. A row is one physical PostgreSQL FK; paired UUID/BIGINT scope constraints are not collapsed here. Cardinality is derived from child-column nullability and uniqueness, not inferred from the workflow.
