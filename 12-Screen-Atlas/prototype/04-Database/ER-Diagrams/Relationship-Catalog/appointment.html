Appointment foreign-key relationships

This register lists every recorded foreign-key constraint owned by child tables in the appointment schema. A row is one physical PostgreSQL FK; paired UUID/BIGINT scope constraints are not collapsed here. Cardinality is derived from child-column nullability and uniqueness, not inferred from the workflow. See the linked diagram for an at-a-glance view and each table contract for full constraints.
