# User journeys

## Customer journey stages — inherited draft

**34 journey stages** are documented as proposed flows. Confirm business policy and recovery outcomes in the linked workflow and decision documents.

- S01 Find the right product
- S02 Understand the item
- S03 Build one shopping cart
- S04 Check the delivery address
- S05 Log in and choose patient when needed
- S06 Confirm what will actually be ordered
- S07 Upload prescription only for P
- S08 Record the order request once
- S09 Review only the P medicines
- S10 Fix a prescription problem
- S11 Approve a changed basket
- S12 Confirm the prescription step is complete
- S13 Choose payment with stock briefly held
- S14 Pay online
- S15 Check a payment with an uncertain result
- S16 Recognise paid or COD order
- S17 Find a store with the full order
- S18 Resolve a stock or delivery problem
- S19 Assign the order to a store
- S20 Pick the exact ordered items
- S21 Fix a picking mistake or shortage
- S22 Check invoice and seal the package
- S23 Assign the rider and hand over the parcel
- S24 Travel to the delivery address
- S25 Verify the recipient and hand over the order
- S26 Finish delivery and provide documents
- S27 Handle a failed delivery
- S28 Cancel safely at the correct stage
- S29 Raise a return wrong item or quality complaint
- S30 Receive and inspect returned items
- S31 Return the correct money once
- S32 Close the order records
- S33 Investigate an unusual problem
- S34 Stop unsafe products immediately
