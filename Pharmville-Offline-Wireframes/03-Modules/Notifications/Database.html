Notifications database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Notification messages and attempts; source domains own business events and customer support remains a separate scope decision. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
