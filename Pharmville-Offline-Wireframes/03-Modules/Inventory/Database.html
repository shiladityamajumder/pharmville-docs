Inventory database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Lots, balances, movement ledger, holds and adjustments; stores/warehouses supply evidence and request movements. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
