Warehouse database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Warehouse geometry, receiving and transfers/procurement; Inventory owns stock ledger and balances. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
