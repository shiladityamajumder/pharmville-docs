Delivery database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Assignments, custody, attempts, OTP/location facts; rider is a scoped client and Finance owns COD money. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
