Customer Accounts database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Profiles, addresses, family associations and consent; delegates credentials and sessions to Authentication. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
