Reporting database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Report definitions, runs and scoped read-only outputs; source domains own data and correction commands. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
