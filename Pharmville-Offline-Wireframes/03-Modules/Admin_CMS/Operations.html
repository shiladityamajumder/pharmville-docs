Unified identity and workspace access

Proposed / Unapproved / Not Implemented — D-003/D-024/D-028. Unified Identity policy is the canonical authentication, scoped authorization, context-switch, step-up and revocation design. Role/workspace matrix defines proposed defaults without granting permissions. Customer and Rider phone OTP and AUTH-01 password/authenticator requirements remain. Account, role and panel are separate: menus and client role strings never authorize an API. No grant means account access only; suspension/expiry deny protected operations. Existing service identities/provider signatures remain separate trust types.
