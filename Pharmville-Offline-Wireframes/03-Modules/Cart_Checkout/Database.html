Cart Checkout database contract

Structure validated; business write assignment Proposed. Cart and checkout intent; Order Management owns orders, Inventory owns holds, Payments owns monetary truth. Read access or a contract surface does not confer write ownership. Exact operation transactions/DTO columns are in APIs; shared audit/outbox/idempotency writes are owner-coordinated infrastructure.
