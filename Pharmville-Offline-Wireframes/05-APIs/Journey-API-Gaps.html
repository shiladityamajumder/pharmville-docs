Scope and clinical invariants

All seven nonempty P/M/O basket combinations retain the documented rule: only combinations containing P require per-line pharmacist review; M/O-only orders skip it. Unknown/unapproved classification blocks checkout. Patient/evidence/revision changes invalidate affected P approval and require explicit revised-basket acceptance, new stock and money revalidation. RX-02 deferred upload remains blocked under D-004/D-006; this pass does not invent its 24-hour deadline behavior or allow review bypass.

WF-013 support-ticket integration and future appointment/insurance/diagnostic/marketplace/membership tables do not justify new launch APIs without D-001/D-018/D-020 approval. Invoice download uses content negotiation on API-029/API-081 (Accept: application/pdf), reusing authenticated private-file access without adding a second invoice route.
