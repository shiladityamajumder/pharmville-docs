Pharmville Business Guide

Pharmville brings customer ordering, pharmacy review, stock, local fulfillment, delivery and finance into one connected process. This guide explains the intended business in everyday language. The underlying product requirements are inherited drafts; the decision register identifies what still needs approval.

Start with the complete business flow, then use the customer experience to follow the screens and choices.

Each domain page explains its normal steps, exceptions, responsible team, visible outcome and pending approvals, followed by technical links. These pages cover all 18 existing module groups. Future healthcare, insurance, membership and marketplace tables do not create approved launch journeys; see scope.
